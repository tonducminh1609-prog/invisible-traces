The project story

From field visits to a conversation

My journey started with a photography trip to Hoi An in the summer, where I truly experienced the life of a visual storyteller for the very first time. Waking up at 3:40 AM to capture the quiet crack of the "blue hour" before dawn, the bustling fish markets at 5 AM, and craft villages coming to life opened my eyes to a completely different world.

During those 10-day lessons, I was fortunate to learn from professional and dedicated photographers—Mrs. Thu, her husband Brett, and Mr. Duc. They had guided me through the explanation of ISO, Aperture, the rule of thirds,..., and accompanied me to notable landmarks in Hoi An to take photographs, such as the ancient town or the pristine craft villages. They also taught me how to choose meaningful shots and continued to support me in curating and printing images for this project.

During those mornings filled with sheer exhaustion and overwhelming sleepiness, yet paired with an extraordinarily buoyant spirit, I realized that the lives of the manual laborers and artisans begin much earlier than ours. Through the lens of a student majoring in chemistry, I also discerned the underlying chemical elements hidden behind each craftsmanship process, which are exceedingly simple yet remarkably fascinating, though known to few. Therefore, through the storytelling knowledge I had acquired, I wanted to use my photographs to narrate the stories of the craft villages, their arduous toil, and the chemistry embedded within them for my close acquaintances.
